SOJOURNER

MAP VIEWER · MINECRAFT · JAVA + BEDROCK

I wanted to see my whole world — not a postcard of the terrain, but everything that's actually in it. Nothing did that. So I wrote this.

// why this exists

I wanted to see everything that's in there.

Every map tool draws the ground and goes quiet. Beautiful hills and oceans — and not a word about the chest you forgot, the villager three biomes over, the spawner under your base. The seed tools go the other way: a flawless generated world you don't actually have, nothing you built, nothing alive. I didn't want either. I wanted to open a map and see the living world — what's really on the disk, blocks and builds and mobs and loot and all. Nobody read the save the way I wanted it read, so I read it myself.

// what it actually shows you

The living world, not just its skin.

Open a save and it draws what's there — the real blocks you placed, the biomes and structures from the seed, the ore veins, the caves. And then the part nobody else bothers with:

  • Every chest and exactly what's in it. Spawners, signs, banners, beehives, brewing stands — all of it, grouped and searchable.
  • Every villager and what they do. Mobs, item frames, minecarts, bosses — placed where they actually stand.
  • Beds, workstations, bells. The bones of a settlement, straight off the save.
  • And the seed side — biomes, structures, slime chunks, ore veins, cave mode, relief.

On Java and Bedrock both. The chest-and-villager depth is the part the fast, pretty renderers never read — and it's the whole reason I built this one.

// how it works

Runs entirely on your machine.

Sojourner is a local desktop app, full stop — no cloud, no login, nothing phoned home. Here's exactly what that gets you:

  • No server. There's nothing to phone home to. Cut it off the network entirely and it works exactly the same.
  • Read-only. It opens your save files to draw them — never writes, moves, or touches anything else on disk.
  • Nothing leaves your machine. No account, no telemetry, no upload, no ads. Just a map of your world, drawn where it already lives.
  • Built by one person. Indie, and unapologetically so — no roadmap meetings, no growth targets, just a tool built to be useful.

This page holds itself to the same bar: zero outside requests, no trackers, a few kilobytes.

// what makes it different

Built for depth, not speed.

This isn't a seed-hunting tool — it shows you the world you already have. And it's not chasing render-benchmark numbers — it's chasing completeness: every chest, every villager, every structure, read straight off your save. That's the depth other viewers skip, and it's the whole reason Sojourner exists.
